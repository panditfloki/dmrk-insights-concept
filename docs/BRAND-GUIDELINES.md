# DMRK Insights: Brand & Website Guidelines

Version 1.0, 26 September 2026. Prepared for client review.

# Evidence, clearly expressed.

A practical identity system for the DMRK Insights website, editorial content and client-facing materials.

## Purpose

Present research with clarity, care and authority. Make methods, assumptions and next actions easy to find. Visual confidence must come from evidence and legibility.

## Status of this guide

The primary blue is client-locked. This guide documents the implemented website system and proposes consistent standards for wider brand use. Typography and new applications remain subject to client approval.

# 01 / Identity & voice

Clear. Evidence-led. Decisive. Use these qualities to judge every page, report and communication.

## Positioning

DMRK Insights provides market intelligence, customer research and strategic consulting that help organisations make confident decisions.

## Writing rules

Lead with the question or decision. Use plain English and specific labels. Explain the method behind a claim. Keep paragraphs short. Use sentence case for headlines and controls. Never use em dashes or inflated claims.

## Evidence before decoration

Do not invent metrics, testimonials, client logos or research outcomes. Label dates, sample sizes, units and sources. Distinguish projections from observed results.

## Calls to action

Use direct, consistent labels: Request research, Browse insights, Talk to an analyst. Keep one main action per section. Make link destinations predictable.

## Example

Prefer: “Test demand before entering a new market.”Avoid: “Unlock unparalleled potential with revolutionary insights.”

# 02 / Core colour palette

Use the exact digital values below. The primary blue must never be approximated or replaced.

- Research blue: #0f67c7. Primary actions, emphasis and the signature CTA band.

- Deep blue: #094f9e. Primary hover state and strong text links.

- Navy: #08223d. Headlines, footer and dark editorial sections.

- Ink: #0d2238. Body text and form entries.

- Muted slate: #60758a. Secondary text and readable placeholders.

- White: #ffffff. Page surfaces and labels on strong blue.

## Colour hierarchy

White and cool blue surfaces carry the page. Navy and ink carry the content. Research blue directs attention. Do not introduce unrelated purple, teal, gold or neon accents.

# 03 / Supporting colours

Supporting colours provide separation, depth and feedback. They are not extra brand accents.

- Accent soft: #e7f1fb. Secondary / inverse hover backgrounds

- Soft: #f2f7fc. Neutral section wash, disabled controls

- Surface blue: #f7fbff. Subtle page sections

- Blue soft: #e8f2fb. Alternate quiet surface

- Line: #d4e3ef. Decorative separators and borders

- Light blue: #5aa7e8. Decoration only; not small text on white

- Error red: #bd1e1e. Errors paired with a written explanation

- On-dark muted: #b9d7f3. Supporting text on navy

- On-dark subtle: #a8c6e4. Secondary labels on navy

- Article ink: #23394f. Long-form reading text

# 04 / Typography

The current website uses Source Serif 4 for headlines and Inter for body copy and controls.

- Hero heading: Source Serif 4 / 700, 34-60 px.

- Section heading: Source Serif 4 / 700, 28-43 px.

- Card heading: Source Serif 4 / 700, 19-23 px.

- Body and controls: Inter / 400-650, 16-17 px.

- Supporting text: Inter / 400-700, 13-14 px.

- Form search input: Inter / 400, 16 px minimum.

## Reading rhythm

Body line height: 1.6. Long-form articles: 1.72. Headlines: about 1.14. Keep prose to 65-75 characters per line. Use fluid sizing across screens; do not shrink body copy to fit.

# 05 / Logo & composition

Use the supplied DMRK logo artwork. Preserve its proportions, letterforms and colour relationships.

## Clear space and minimum size

For new applications, reserve at least one quarter of the symbol height around the lockup. Recommended minimum: 32 px symbol on screens, 10 mm in print. Use the dedicated icon asset for browser favicons.

## Approved use

Use the blue symbol on white or pale surfaces. On navy, use the supplied symbol with a white wordmark. Keep the original artwork intact. Never stretch, rotate, outline, recolour or add a glow to the logo.

## Layout system

Use a 4 px spacing base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64 px. Align content to shared page gutters. Website gutters scale from 20 to 112 px, with a 1760 px maximum wrapper. Let content wrap naturally.

## Shape and depth

Use 8 px standard corners, 6 px for small elements and 14 px for feature panels. Buttons use pill corners. Keep shadows soft and navy-tinted; no heavy black shadows or decorative outlines.

# 06 / Buttons & interaction

Each variant owns both its background and its label colour. Never apply a dark hover background to every button.

- Primary: default #0f67c7 / #ffffff; hover #094f9e / #ffffff (background / text).

- Secondary: default #ffffff / #08223d; hover #e7f1fb / #094f9e (background / text).

- On blue / navy: default #ffffff / #08223d; hover #e7f1fb / #08223d (background / text).

- Disabled: default #f2f7fc / #60758a; hover #f2f7fc / #60758a (background / text).

# 07 / Forms, content & motion

Use consistent behaviours alongside consistent colours. Interaction is part of the identity.

## Inputs and dropdowns

Use white surfaces, ink text, visible labels and muted-slate placeholders. Focus uses the brand-blue border. Category menus open with search at the top, filter immediately, support arrow keys and Enter, and close with Escape. Show a useful empty state.

## Optional market information

Market prices remain collapsed by default. The Show control reveals the widget; Hide closes it. Load third-party market content only after the reader asks for it. The research content remains the priority.

## Editorial imagery

Prefer relevant, credible sector photography and clearly sourced research visuals. Avoid generic handshakes, ornamental AI imagery and misleading data graphics. Use a consistent crop, provide meaningful alt text and confirm asset licensing.

## Charts and tables

Lead with blue, then navy and light blue for supporting series. Label values directly; never rely on colour alone. State the unit, period, source and sample. Do not invent a multi-colour palette for each report.

## Mobile and motion

Stack content cleanly. Keep menus scrollable, labels visible and actions reachable. Use native scrolling. Reduce or remove nonessential animation when the reader requests reduced motion.

# 08 / Brand applications

Apply one identity across reports, presentations, email and social content. These are recommended extensions for client approval.

## Reports and proposals

Use a white A4 page, a restrained logo lockup and a single blue heading or rule. Set body text in Inter at 10-11 pt, with 1.5 line spacing. Keep 16-20 mm margins. Use Source Serif 4 for major section titles. Put the document title, date, version and page number in predictable positions.

## Presentations

Use 16:9 slides with generous margins. Lead each slide with a clear takeaway. Use white content slides, navy dividers and blue for evidence or the main action. Avoid placing body text over photography. Label every chart and make footnotes readable.

## Social and editorial previews

Start from 1080 × 1080 px for square artwork or 1200 × 630 px for a landscape article preview. Keep text and logo inside a 6% safe margin. Use one message, one supporting visual and a short source line. Adapt the composition instead of stretching a template.

## Email and signatures

Use live text, a small logo and one clear link. Use system sans-serif where custom fonts are unavailable. Keep the signature factual: name, role, DMRK Insights and verified contact details. Do not turn every email into an image.

## Icons and delivery

Use simple, consistent line icons around 1.8-2 px at 24 px. Match icon colour to its label. Pair unfamiliar icons with text. Deliver vector SVG for logos, PDF for documents, and sRGB PNG/JPEG for raster artwork. Retain editable source files.

# 09 / Accessibility & release checks

These are repeatable checks for every new page and component, not a claim of full-site accessibility certification.

- Primary label: 5.55:1 (#ffffff on #0f67c7).

- Primary hover label: 8.00:1 (#ffffff on #094f9e).

- Secondary hover label: 7.00:1 (#094f9e on #e7f1fb).

- Inverse hover label: 14.06:1 (#08223d on #e7f1fb).

- Body text: 16.12:1 (#0d2238 on #ffffff).

- Placeholder / muted: 4.76:1 (#60758a on #ffffff).

- Footer supporting text: 10.77:1 (#b9d7f3 on #08223d).

- Error text: 6.23:1 (#bd1e1e on #ffffff).

## Before publishing

Check default, hover, pressed, focus, loading and disabled states. Verify real links, menu transitions, category selection and clear filters. Test desktop, keyboard and narrow mobile screens. Check text contrast, zoom, wrapping and empty/error states.

## Consistency ownership

Use the shared design tokens and component variants. Make a deliberate system change before adding a new colour or font. Preview changes locally, review them, then publish the approved build.
